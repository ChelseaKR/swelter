# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

swelter exists to give frontline neighborhoods data about the heat and air they live in. The
project only works if the people who host sensors, write code, and read the map are treated with
respect. Everyone who takes part — contributors, maintainers, hosting collectives, and people
filing issues — is welcome regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and
expression, immigration status, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## The standard, in short

We adopt the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1** as the standard for behavior in this project.
The full text is published at <https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.
In plain terms:

- Be considerate, welcoming, and patient, especially with newcomers and non-engineers — many
  people here are community organizers, not developers.
- Assume good faith, give and accept constructive feedback gracefully, and center what is best for
  the community served, not for any individual.
- Do not harass, demean, or exclude. Personal attacks, sustained disruption, and publishing other
  people's private information are not acceptable.

Because of what this project is, two project-specific expectations carry the same weight:

- **Protect the people behind the sensors.** Do not seek out, share, or pressure anyone to disclose
  the precise location of a node or the identity of a host. Coarse, grid-snapped locations are the
  default for a reason (see `docs/governance.md`).
- **Do not weaponize the data.** Contributions or conduct intended to use swelter to surveil,
  target, or profile people are out of scope and unwelcome.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions — and in
public spaces when an individual is representing the project.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately to the maintainer, Chelsea
Kelly-Reif, through the private channel described in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md). Reports are
handled discreetly. The maintainer will review and respond, and may warn, temporarily restrict, or
permanently remove anyone whose behavior is judged harmful, following the Contributor Covenant's
enforcement guidelines.

This is a small, independent project; enforcement is handled directly and in good faith rather than
through a formal committee.
